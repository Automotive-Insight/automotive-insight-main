Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Automotive Insight (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website at automotiveinsight.com.au. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information we collect

When you submit a booking request through our website, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your vehicle registration, make, model, and year
  • A description of the service or issue you’ve described

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your service enquiry and arrange your booking. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data storage

Booking enquiries submitted through this website are stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is retained for the purpose of managing your booking and service history.

Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics services.
